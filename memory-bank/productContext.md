# Product Context

## Features

- **League Settings:** Users can enter and configure their fantasy football league settings.
- **Player Information:** Users can see all the players in the league.
- **Team Setup:** Users can define the teams in their league.
- **Draft Assistance:** The app will provide draft advice.
- **In-Season Advice:** The app will provide in-season advice.
- **Vercel AI SDK Integration:** The app will use the Vercel AI SDK for LLM communication.
- **API Integration:** The app will integrate with various fantasy football APIs or MCP servers.
